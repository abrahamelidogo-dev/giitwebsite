How to Register for SIWES at GiiT

Follow the steps below carefully to complete your SIWES registration:

  1. Visit the GiiT office at your nearest branch (Maiduguri).
  2. Present your school SIWES placement letter to the admin officer.
  3. Fill out the GiiT SIWES Intake Form.
  4. Submit two passport photographs and a copy of your student ID.
  5. Select your preferred training track:
    1. Web Development
    2. Graphics & Multimedia
    3. Software Development
    4. Networking & IT Support
  6. Receive your GiiT SIWES ID card and begin training the following Monday.

GiiT ICT Glossary — Terms Every Student Must Know

HTML
HyperText Markup Language — the standard language used to create and structure content on the web.
CSS
Cascading Style Sheets — used to style and visually present HTML elements.
SIWES
Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme — a Federal Government of Nigeria program that exposes students to industry work environment.
GiiT
Global Institute of ICT Training — a premier ICT training center in Northern Nigeria.
Domain Name
The human-readable web address used to identify a website, e.g., giitcomputer.com.
Server
A computer system that stores, manages, and delivers web content to client devices over the internet.
